### What's new in 0.8.0

A focus timer on the bar.
- Click the stopwatch for a Pomodoro: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break, over and over
- Type what you're working on before you start; the bar shows it with the time left, like "Write the report · 24:59"
- Start, Pause and Reset; pausing keeps the time you've done
- During a break the stopwatch turns your theme's colour
