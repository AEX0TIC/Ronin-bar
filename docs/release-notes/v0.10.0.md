### What's new in 0.10.0

The bar where you want it, and never in the way.
- Settings → Bar position: Top, Bottom, Left or Right
- On the left or right the bar stands upright: Ronin mark and clock at the top, your apps, then the controls (hover them for percentages)
- Popups and the panel open next to the bar, on whichever side it is
- Auto-hide now only gives way when a window needs the space: with nothing under it, the bar stays and covers nothing
- When a window reaches under it, the bar hides completely, so it never covers that window's buttons; rest the mouse at the edge for half a second to bring it back
