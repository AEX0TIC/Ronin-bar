### What's new in 0.11.0

More ways to time your work.
- Choose Pomodoro or Timer in the stopwatch popup
- Pomodoro 25 + 5 or 50 + 10: when focus ends, the break starts by itself, then focus again
- Timer: set any number of hours and minutes; it stops when the time is up and the bar shows "Done"
- Timer at 0 h 0 min counts up, as a stopwatch, with no limit
- Times of an hour or more show hours, like 1:30:00
- Your choice is remembered for next time
