### What's new in 0.6.2

Bluetooth and Wi-Fi without lag.
- Bluetooth: Windows reports every device change (paired, connected, disconnected, removed) as it happens, so the list is always current and never "did not answer in time"
- Search stops by itself after about 25 seconds or when the popup closes, and "searching…" never shows while Bluetooth is off
- Connect and disconnect show the new state the moment you click
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth switches react instantly and no longer flicker back while Windows is switching
- The launcher is just your apps and the power button
