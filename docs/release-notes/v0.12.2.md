### What's new in 0.12.2

A compact, cleaner player.
- The player popup is a smaller card: the picture with the title, artist and app beside it
- Long titles end neatly with "…" (hover for the full title)
- Little bars next to the app's name move while it plays
- A thin progress bar sits between the two times and grows when you point at it
- Smaller previous, play / pause and next buttons, all fitting inside the card
