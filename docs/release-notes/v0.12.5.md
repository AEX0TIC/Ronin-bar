### What's new in 0.12.5

Updates download smoothly.
- The download no longer sits at "0.0 MB": it shows "Connecting to GitHub…", then "Downloading 1.2 of 5.5 MB (22%)", updated smoothly
- A slow connection is never cut off halfway; a dead one is noticed within seconds
- A dropped download is tried again by itself, up to 3 times
- If it still fails, the error comes with **Try again** and **Download from GitHub** buttons
