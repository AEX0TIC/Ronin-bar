### What's new in 0.12.1

The bar survives sleep and wake.
- A small watchdog keeps the bar running: if it ever ends unexpectedly (some graphics drivers drop it while the PC sleeps), it starts again within a second or two
- While the screen is still waking up, the bar keeps its last size instead of shrinking to nothing
- Timers and the media progress bar stay correct right after the PC wakes
- If the bar is ever down for a moment, Windows' own taskbar shows until it is back
