### What's new in 0.5.1

Cleaner bar and easier pins.
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and quick settings each get their own icon and popup
- Bluetooth popup with the on/off switch and your paired devices
- Launcher: a Pin / Unpin button on every app, and an × on pinned apps; right-click a pinned app on the bar to unpin it
- The Ronin panel keeps just Wallpapers, Themes and Settings
- Web apps such as Netflix show their own icon and name instead of their browser's
