### What's new in 0.7.0

A cleaner, easier launcher.
- Only apps you can open: uninstallers, manuals, web links, setup tools and driver helpers are left out
- Programs that never added a Start-menu entry are now listed too, found in Windows' list of installed software
- Most used: the apps you open most often, right under your pinned apps
- Smarter search: the start of a name, any word in it, or its initials (vsc finds Visual Studio Code)
- Arrow keys choose an app and Enter opens it
- Right-click any app to pin it, hide it from the launcher, or show it in its folder; "show hidden" brings hidden apps back
- The update popup shows each release's What's new as a tidy list
