### What's new in 0.9.0

A bar that stays out of your way.
- Auto-hide: when the mouse leaves the bar, it slides up out of view; touch the top edge of the screen and it slides back
- With auto-hide, windows use the whole screen
- It stays while a popup or the panel is open
- Fullscreen videos, games and presentations hide the bar completely, now including browser fullscreen
- Prefer it always showing? Untick Settings → Hide the bar when not in use
