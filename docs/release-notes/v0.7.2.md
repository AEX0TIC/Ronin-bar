### What's new in 0.7.2

The bar always fits your screen.
- The bar reads the main monitor's real scale (Settings → Display → Scale) instead of the one Windows remembered from sign-in
- When the scale changes or a monitor is plugged in or out, the bar keeps its proper height and width while it runs
- The Ronin panel and all popups open in the right place again (the panel opened in the middle after a scale change)
