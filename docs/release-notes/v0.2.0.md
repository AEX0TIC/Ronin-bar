### What's new in 0.2.0

Ronin Bar replaces the Windows taskbar.
- The Windows taskbar is hidden while the bar runs, and comes back when you quit or uninstall
- A new, classier look, and the Ronin logo: an open ensō cut by a katana
- Every setting has its own button on the bar: wallpapers, themes, apps, settings, power
- Ronin original wallpapers, drawn by the bar itself, ready on first start and offline
