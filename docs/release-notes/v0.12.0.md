### What's new in 0.12.0

A media notch on the bar.
- When music or a video plays, the clock's pill smoothly grows into a notch: picture, title and artist, and bars that move with the music
- Long titles glide sideways; when playback stops, the notch shrinks back to the clock
- Click it for the player: large picture, a progress bar that glides with the music (click or drag to jump), previous, play / pause and next
- Middle-click the notch to play or pause
- Works with Spotify, YouTube in your browser, Netflix, VLC, Media Player and anything that shows in Windows' media box, and stays in step with it
