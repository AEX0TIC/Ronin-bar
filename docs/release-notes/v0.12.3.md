### What's new in 0.12.3

The bar stays out of maximised windows.
- With a maximised window in front, the bar stays hidden: it never comes down over the window, not even when the mouse touches the edge
- It comes back as soon as the desktop or a window that is not maximised is in front
- Want the old way? Settings → "Over a maximised window, bring the bar back when the mouse rests at the edge"
