### What's new in 0.8.1

Wallpapers download and change at once.
- A wallpaper you click starts downloading straight away (it used to wait 1.5 seconds first)
- The bar no longer pauses while Windows applies a new wallpaper; it happens in the background
- A category's "get" downloads 4 pictures at a time, and each appears the moment it is in
- The panel updates as soon as a download finishes, instead of up to a second later
